# Module 1 — Inception of Open-Source EDA, OpenLANE and Sky130 PDK

## Overview

This module introduces the foundations of open-source digital ASIC physical design, including how software communicates with hardware, SoC design, OpenLANE, the Sky130 PDK, open-source EDA tools, and the beginning of the RTL-to-GDS flow.

## Topics Covered

1. How to Talk to Computers
2. SoC Design and OpenLANE
3. Getting Familiar with Open-Source EDA Tools
4. OpenLANE and the Sky130 PDK
5. Technology-specific and tool-specific concepts
6. OpenLANE directory structure and design preparation
7. Simplified and detailed RTL-to-GDS flow
8. Synthesis and gate-level netlist generation
9. Synthesis-result review and characterization
10. Basic timing-related parameters and clock settings

## Learning Outcome

After this module, the learner should understand the role of open-source EDA tools and the Sky130 PDK, the major stages of the OpenLANE RTL-to-GDS flow, and how synthesis produces a gate-level netlist for subsequent physical-design stages.
