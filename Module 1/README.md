# Module 1 — Inception of Open-Source EDA, OpenLANE and Sky130 PDK

## Overview

This module introduces the foundations of open-source digital ASIC design and the beginning of the RTL-to-GDS physical-design flow. It covers how to talk to computers, SoC design, OpenLANE, the Sky130 PDK, open-source EDA tools, technology-specific and tool-specific concepts, synthesis, and basic design/timing parameters.

## Topics Covered

1. **How to Talk to Computers**
2. **SoC Design and OpenLANE**
3. **Getting Familiar with Open-Source EDA Tools**
4. OpenLANE and the Sky130 PDK
5. Technology-specific and tool-specific concepts
6. OpenLANE flow and its major stages
7. RTL-to-GDS flow
8. Synthesis and netlist generation
9. Basic timing-related calculations
10. Chip-area and clock-setting information
